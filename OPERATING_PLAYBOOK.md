# Vave Workflow Store — Master Operating Playbook

**Mission:** sell verified ready-made Make workflows through Gumroad, grow through creators, nurture opt-in subscribers and offer carefully scoped implementation services.

## Current evidence (Make read-only check, 2 Nov 2026)
- 17 Vave development scenarios saved, all inactive.
- No finished product has completed the four-item release gate.
- 17 listing drafts and one custom storefront candidate prepared; seller account not confirmed deployed.
- 28 public creator research candidates, not contacted / not signed based on source workbook.
- Client CRM for the store begins empty rather than borrowing unrelated agency results.

## Weekly executive cadence

**Monday — Product reality check**: confirm one release bottleneck, review Make compatibility safely, track blueprint/import/QA evidence and update customer scope.

**Tuesday — Partner qualification**: add a batch of credible niche creators, inspect recent content and confirm public business contact routes. Remove strong conflicts. Prioritize high-quality fit over vanity follower counts.

**Wednesday — Client acquisition**: research targeted businesses by repeatable pain and prepare specific, opt-out-respecting intros. Log actual attempts rather than counting uncontacted leads.

**Thursday — Content & email**: write one useful automation explainer, refine a real demo asset if available, keep Vave Dispatch useful and accurate.

**Friday — Revenue & operations**: review true sales and refunds only when connected; analyze support questions, software costs and affiliate attribution without fabricated analytics.

## Release rule
No blueprint export, clean import and runtime evidence → **no published product**. No buyer-facing claims that go beyond the actual saved scenario modules.

## Outreach rule
Public, credible business contact channels only. No guessed contacts, deceptive testimonials, unsolicited bulk blasting, fake urgency or disregard for opt-outs. Tailor each offer to a documented need.

## Data rule
Local HQ data is browser-private to the device but not a secure, synchronized database. Do not expose sensitive contacts or credentials on a public GitHub Pages deployment.
