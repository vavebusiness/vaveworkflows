A few things worth knowing

Clear answers.

Does Content Repurposer edit or publish videos?

No. It generates written social post drafts, video scripts, a newsletter draft and a suggested review queue from original text. It does not ingest video URLs, edit clips, schedule posts, or publish automatically.

Do I need my own Make account?

Yes. The intended buyer package requires a Make workspace and your own supported Gemini connection. API costs are separate. Clean-account import verification is a release requirement.

Is the workflow available to buy now?

Not from this preview. The Gumroad product is not yet confirmed published. The buy control will appear only when a real checkout URL and customer download have been verified.

How does affiliate payment work?

Approved partners will use their actual Gumroad affiliate links. The proposed commission is 60% of qualifying attributed sales, subject to the platform's payout eligibility, fees, refunds and final program terms.
