Two commercially different paths

Creator affiliates vs. strategic partnerships

01 / Creator distribution

Education → tracked product purchase

Small AI, creator-operations, and no-code educators recommend a specific self-serve workflow they have genuinely tried. Direct Gumroad affiliate links attribute qualifying sales.

Proposal: 60% standard affiliate commission for eligible attributed workflow product sales, subject to Gumroad configuration and payout eligibility.

02 / Strategic referrals

Business introduction → qualified opportunity

Agencies and consultants introduce relevant business buyers or co-market tutorials. Product referrals can use Gumroad; custom implementation projects need a separately agreed scope, client ownership, support and referral terms.

Proposal: quote and agree each project; no automatic payout promise or double commission.

Launch sequence

Four-week pilot

Week 01

Requalify and listen

Recheck the 28 research records; identify A-tier creators; send a maximum of five manually personalized **feedback requests**. No finished-product claims.

Week 02

Validate one hero workflow

Technical review, blueprint export, import test when authorized, delivery ZIP, demo, terms, margin and seller eligibility checks.

Week 03 · after release gate

Activate 3–5 partners

Invite qualified creators, provide direct Gumroad affiliate links, factual kits, disclosure guidance, and individual attribution tracking.

Week 04 · after release gate

Review actual results

Review authentic posts, tracked orders and refunds, install tickets and creator feedback. Decide whether to expand, improve or pause.

If release remains blocked, Weeks 3–4 turn into feedback and content-asset preparation only. They are not assumed to happen on a fixed calendar date.
