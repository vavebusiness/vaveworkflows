# Client acquisition — workflow implementation buyers

This client desk belongs to Vave Workflow Store. It is separate from the other Vave Automations agency CRM.

## Three offers to validate
1. **Creator content ops:** simplify transcript→draft→review handoffs.
2. **Invoice intake:** reviewable extraction into structured records, with human approval.
3. **Property document review:** extraction of rent-roll field drafts, not automatic underwriting.

## Research target (starting hypothesis)
- Research ~30 businesses per week in one vertical.
- Qualify 15 relevant organizations.
- Contact ~10–15 with specific, respectful, legitimate business introductions per week.
- Log all outcomes, opt-outs and bounces.
- Revise target based on response quality, not raw volume.

## Opportunity SOP
1. Verify company name, business use case, source and appropriate public business contact.
2. Identify one painful repetitive task that actually fits the available workflow.
3. Personalize a brief outreach; never promise proven time savings without a verified case.
4. If interested, ask what tools, formats, volumes, review steps and safeguards are involved.
5. Agree a small bounded pilot with acceptance criteria before quoting full deployment.
6. Track proposal, acceptance, actual delivery and support; no fabricated testimonials.

## Pipeline stages
Research → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Proposal → Won/Lost.

## Controls
Respect marketing rules and unsubscribe requests; do not scrape private contact information. Do not automate bulk sends without careful legal/account authorization. Keep customer files and passwords out of public GitHub Pages.
