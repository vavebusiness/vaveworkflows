# Vave Partner Outreach & Onboarding Kit

This copy is for manual review and personalization. Do not send to a list blindly, use invented familiarity, or imply a product is tested when it is not.

## 1. Pre-launch feedback invitation (appropriate before QA)

**Subject:** Quick workflow idea for your [specific tutorial audience]

Hi [creator name],

I came across your [specific public tutorial] about [specific topic]. The part about [relevant observation] made me think of something I'm developing for creators who [audience pain point].

I'm putting together Vave Workflow Store, with ready-to-import Make workflows and clear setup guides. Our first candidate is a hook-planning system, which is still in development and hasn't been released.

Would you be open to sharing which part of your audience's content workflow is the most repetitive? Not pitching a finished product — I want to make sure the first release is actually useful.

Thanks,  
Vave Workflow Store

## 2. Post-QA founder invitation (only after verified product and terms)

**Subject:** Interested in demonstrating [verified product] to your audience?

Hi [creator name],

Your [specific video/post] on [topic] is closely related to a practical workflow we've now finished validating: [product name]. It helps users [one accurately verified outcome] using [real prerequisites].

We're inviting a small group of creators to independently try it and, if they genuinely find it useful, share it with their audience. The proposed [approved]% affiliate commission is tracked through a direct Gumroad link; we provide a real demo, installation guide, and accurate feature FAQ.

If it's relevant, I can send the details and a review copy. There's no requirement to give a positive review.

Thanks,  
Vave Workflow Store

## 3. Light follow-up (4–5 days later)

Hi [name], checking in once on the workflow idea I mentioned. I thought of your [specific audience / original tutorial] because [short authentic reason]. If this isn't relevant, no worries and I won't follow up further. Thanks!

## 4. Final follow-up (about 10–12 days after first contact)

Hi [name], just closing the loop. If you'd ever want to explore a practical Make workflow demo for [audience use case], feel free to reach out. Otherwise I'll leave it here. Thanks!

## 5. Qualification / interest questions

1. Who in your audience regularly buys tools or setup guides rather than only following AI news?
2. Which repetitive workflow do people ask you to explain or set up?
3. Are you already offering a competing template or course that would make an affiliation awkward?
4. Would a 30–60s walkthrough, a longer tutorial or a newsletter feature be most useful?
5. What would you need to verify personally before recommending a product?

## 6. Founder cohort activation checklist

- [ ] Partner is genuinely interested; contact/source/consent recorded
- [ ] Seller is eligible and payout account properly configured
- [ ] Product has blueprint and verification evidence
- [ ] Conditions of commission, duration and covered products explicitly agreed
- [ ] Partner joined Gumroad with valid payout information
- [ ] Direct affiliate invitation issued for the right product(s)
- [ ] Partner has received their specific tracking link and confirmed it works
- [ ] Partner receives demo, setup guide and accurate features/limitations
- [ ] Partner knows support contact and how to request claim corrections
- [ ] Post clearly states affiliate relationship in content and caption when appropriate
- [ ] Date and link of live post added to HQ CRM
- [ ] Actual sales and refunds are read from platform evidence, not estimated from views

## 7. Creator promotion menu

- **Educational micro-demo:** one specific workflow problem → real verified screen recording → implementation requirements → affiliate disclosure + attributed link.
- **Short tutorial:** show how to import, connect apps and validate an output from your own honest experience.
- **Comparison video:** manual approach vs verified workflow features; never invent time savings.
- **Newsletter feature:** one valuable free tip, truthful tool demo, optional affiliate link, disclosure beside link.
- **Story / carousel:** problem, workflow architecture, audience suitability, requirements, call to action and disclosure.

## 8. Approval rubric for promotional claims

Allowed after proof: 'The workflow generates candidate hooks from the inputs you provide' *if that behavior is successfully verified*.  
Not allowed: 'Runs your entire creator business', 'always goes viral', 'automatically posts everywhere', 'guaranteed sales', or fabricated customer quotes.  
Also avoid: pretending a user has tested the product when they have not.

## 9. Strategic partnership qualification

Ask separately from the affiliate pitch:
- What concrete buyer segment or implementation project can the partner bring?
- Is this a product referral or paid implementation project?
- Who owns the client relationship, scope, billing, refunds, support, and customer data?
- Is there an overlapping vendor/affiliate commission, and is it allowed?
- What is the smallest safe pilot with written acceptance criteria?

Do not treat nonbinding conversation as signed distribution or forecast revenue.
