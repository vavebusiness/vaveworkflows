# Affiliate & partner growth — operating standard

**Detailed pilot:** [`PARTNER_PROGRAM_V1.md`](PARTNER_PROGRAM_V1.md) · **Outreach and onboarding:** [`PARTNER_OUTREACH_AND_ONBOARDING.md`](PARTNER_OUTREACH_AND_ONBOARDING.md) · **Interactive model:** [`../systems/partner-pilot.html`](../systems/partner-pilot.html)


## North star
Turn a small set of relevant creators into a reliable distribution channel for **verified** workflows. 28 research leads currently exist; zero signed affiliate relationships are assumed.

## Two distinct pipelines
- **Creator affiliates:** audience education, tracked sales and documented commissions.
- **Strategic partners:** referrals, white-label, distribution and technical co-delivery relationships.

## Weekly research block
1. Find 20–30 relevant accounts within AI tutorials, no-code, creator operations or small-business education.
2. Exclude obvious conflicts, inactive pages, private/inferred contact addresses, suspicious engagement and audiences with no product fit.
3. Verify their current activity on public sources, the channel to contact them, and which specific product they could honestly demo.
4. Grade A / B / C based on actual fit and reachability, not follower count alone.
5. Move only strong fits to the qualified queue; record source and next action.

## Outreach block
- Start with five highly specific pitches, not hundreds of generic messages.
- Reference a real public tutorial and one product problem. Offer independent access to a properly tested demo.
- Explain commercial terms clearly, including eligibility, refunds and disclosure requirements.
- Do not claim previous customers, returns or conversion results without evidence.
- If no reply, make a limited polite follow-up when appropriate; honor opt-outs.

## Partner activation checklist
- [ ] Verified workflow demo available
- [ ] Accurate features and technical requirements
- [ ] Commission economics signed off
- [ ] Valid Gumroad affiliate link created
- [ ] Approved creative kit (video source, screenshots, copy)
- [ ] Correct sponsorship/affiliate disclosures
- [ ] No secrets, personal customer data or unlicensed content shared
- [ ] Attribution and reporting approach recorded

## Weekly KPI review
Source leads; qualified leads; outreach attempts; meaningful replies; active partners; visits attributable to each partner; verified purchases; refund rate; support burden. Never count research entries as signed partners or views as sales.
