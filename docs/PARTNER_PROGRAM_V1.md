# Vave Workflow Store — Partner Program v1.0

**Planning date:** 2 November 2026  
**Owner:** Vave Workflow Store  
**State:** Planning, not activated.  
**Do not interpret this plan as evidence of any sales, signed affiliates, sent outreach, or validated product.**

## Executive decision

Begin with **one product, a small creator cohort, and tracked sales**, not 17 untested products and a giant affiliate army. The first product candidate is **Vave Viral Hook Lab**; the launch gate requires a real Make blueprint export, fresh-account import, sample input/output tests, support instructions, and approved Gumroad listing. No scenario executions or Make credits were used to prepare this plan.

We will maintain **two different partner pipelines**:

1. **Creator affiliates**: content creators who recommend a verified self-serve Make workflow to buyers through their own content using direct Gumroad affiliate links. Commission is tied to completed sales, adjusted for refunds and platform policies.
2. **Strategic partners**: agencies, consultants, communities or educators who may refer businesses, make introductions, co-create educational content, or arrange implementation work. Product-sale referrals can use Gumroad affiliate links; service/project referral terms must be agreed separately and are not automatically handled by Gumroad.

Do not combine research candidates, contacted prospects, interested respondents, approved affiliates and revenue-generating partners in one stage. Each is a different evidence level.

## What Vave promises a partner

- One concrete outcome to teach or demonstrate, not generic 'AI makes you rich' positioning.
- A genuinely usable workflow plus documentation **only after verification**.
- Short honest promo kit: screen recording, workflow overview, prerequisites, setup time estimate *only after measured*, 3 script angles, feature limits, common objections, FAQs, and plain-language disclosure guidance.
- A unique attributed link from Gumroad. Direct affiliate accounts are added only with their legitimate Gumroad-registered email and payout settings ready.
- No obligation to misrepresent hands-on experience, no promises about sales numbers, no fake case studies.
- Clear support, refund and technical ownership boundaries.

## Offer structure — proposal, not approved

| Model | Commission / benefit | When to use | Caveats |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Standard creator | 25% of qualifying product price | Default direct affiliate | Seller verifies actual fees, payout eligibility, and current settings |
| Founding creator | 30% for 60 days after agreed launch | First 3–5 qualified partners | Limited duration and exact covered products documented; don't promise until account supports it |
| Proven creator | Up to 35% after 10+ nonrefunded attributed sales in a month | Only after profitable pilot | Upgrade prospectively after explicit approval; don't silently change terms |
| Agency sales referral | Normally same 25% direct-affiliate arrangement | Resells/introduces a pre-made product | Use Gumroad links for product-sale attribution |
| Custom implementation referral | Case-by-case agreement (e.g., 10% of collected first-project service fees) | Agency/consultant introduces a client | Separate written agreement, clear exclusions, refund triggers, legal/contract review, no double commission |
| White-label implementation | Individually quoted project scope or revenue share | Technical co-delivery | Never imply a Gumroad affiliate link handles service-contract payments |

**Do not recruit under binding terms or promise payouts until a legally eligible account owner has confirmed Gumroad account/payout eligibility and commercial terms.** Gumroad's Terms require legal capacity to form a contract.

## Financial guardrail

Current Gumroad help lists direct-link fees as 10% + $0.50 plus card processing of 2.9% + $0.30, subject to payment route and other fees. Gumroad states affiliate-related fees are split proportionally between seller and affiliate. For conservative planning, subtract **all estimated direct-sale transaction fees from the seller's share** rather than depending on the fee split.

For illustrative $29 product / 25% affiliate commission, estimated gross sale $29, affiliate share $7.25, estimated combined platform/processing fees $4.54, **conservative seller contribution $17.21** before refunds, support, business taxes, or any other costs. Actual payouts may differ. Do not use this estimate as a promise of partner earnings.

A $5 discount reduces sale amount, affiliate payout, and contribution. Test discount stacking before distributing codes. Avoid paying high affiliate commission plus a deep coupon without checking margins.

## Target niches (in priority order)

1. **Creator operations educators:** show a specific workflow that saves planning or scripting work.
2. **Beginner Make / no-code educators:** high product fit, but distinguish them from competitors selling almost the same template.
3. **Content systems operators:** theme-page managers, editors, and independent marketers who demonstrate practical tools.
4. **Service-business educators:** only when the featured product clearly matches an audience pain point.
5. **Large generic 'AI news' accounts:** deprioritize unless their audience shows actual workflow-buying intent.

The existing HQ has **28 researched prospects, zero verified signed affiliate relationships**. Evidence sources should be revisited before contacting anybody. Some suggested leads are strategic collaborators rather than high-confidence affiliates if they already sell overlapping workflows.

## Outreach process and SLA

### Gate 0 — Pre-launch discovery (safe now)
- Research 30 leads and review the existing 28 records; deduplicate and reverify contact sources.
- Qualify the best 10; ask no more than 5 initial prospects for feedback on a future, accurately described product. No paid offer, checkout link, commission guarantee or assertion that the workflow is proven.
- Log their response, objections, product fit, whether they want a demo later, and opt-out.

### Gate 1 — Product is genuinely launchable
- Product QA owner reviews the release evidence.
- Seller account, affiliate terms, support, and refund policies approved.
- At this point create a working Gumroad draft and invite the first 3–5 suitable creators.
- Share their own valid affiliate link only after it exists, and record which product and commission applies.

### Gate 2 — Partner trial
- Provide approved **real** demo assets and self-service instructions.
- Ask for one educational post or tutorial (creator may choose format and give honest opinion); avoid scripted fake testimonials.
- Review facts and disclosure on the creator's proposed claims, not their subjective opinion.
- Wait for Gumroad data before declaring success.

### Gate 3 — Measure, improve, expand
- Compare distinct attributed purchases and refunds by creator. Use clicks as supplementary signals; cookie/privacy limitations can prevent perfect attribution.
- Interview partners who got clicks but few sales; inspect the actual product page and audience fit.
- Keep the best-performing angle, improve documentation, and recruit the next cohort only after support is manageable.

### Suggested follow-ups
- Day 0: personalized first note; one clear purpose.
- Day 4–5: one short value-add follow-up (new demo preview or a relevant teaching idea, if genuinely available).
- Day 10–12: final polite follow-up if appropriate.
- Then close or nurture; never keep chasing uninterested creators. Suppress opt-outs.

## Metrics to record weekly

**Research:** researched, verified public contact, A/B qualified, outreach drafted.  
**Engagement:** first outreach actually sent, responses, interested, declined, needs follow-up, opt-outs.  
**Activation:** approved, registered direct affiliate, unique link verified, has promo kit, actual content published.  
**Sales:** link clicks if independently measurable, Gumroad attributed sales, gross order amount, discounts, refunds, paid commissions, conservative contribution, support tickets.  
**Quality:** content claims corrections, install errors, refund reasons, unresolved partner questions.

North-star partnership metric: **net contribution from genuine referred sales after refunds, fees and support**. Initial actionable leading indicators are qualified partner replies and valid demo readiness — not follower count.

## Roles

- **Owner/operator:** approve offers, invitations, policy, payout/legal compliance, private CRM, outreach messages, pricing.
- **Research:** qualify evidence and maintain stage hygiene.
- **Product QA:** validates real blueprints, safe sample data, import, requirements and documented limitations.
- **Partner success:** prepares kits, onboarding and support, and logs verified partner activity.
- **Finance/operations:** reconciles Gumroad sales/refunds, fees and contribution. Avoid sharing sensitive payouts or contact info on a public static website.

## Failure scenarios / decision rules

- **No replies from 10 excellent prospects:** fix pitch and segment, not simply send 500 generic DMs.
- **Interest but no activations:** improve proof, demo, setup difficulty and commission clarity.
- **Clicks without sales:** inspect product page conversion, actual prerequisites, price and buyer questions.
- **Sales with heavy refunds/support:** stop scaling, fix product and onboarding first.
- **One creator drives all sales:** retain them but diversify to reduce single-channel reliance.
- **Partner makes misleading claims:** request correction/pause affiliate and stop using their content; document facts.
- **No product QA:** keep all collaborations in feedback/research stage.
