# Vave Dispatch — consent-based lifecycle email

The HQ contains seven editable draft emails. None has been sent or scheduled by the HQ.

## Segments
- Followers: free resources, tips, launches, occasional coupons.
- Buyers: import/setup help and relevant updates.
- Approved affiliates: onboarding, product demos and disclosures.

## Draft sequences
- Subscriber: welcome at signup → practical workflow tip at +2 days → free resource at +5 days.
- Buyer: correct download/install guide and approved support route; later useful check-in.
- Affiliate: policy + tracking instructions + verified product creative resources.

## Cadence
Prefer 1–2 high-quality emails per month after initial onboarding, then adjust to actual engagement and unsubscribes. Promotions should not crowd out real utility.

## Gumroad constraints
Gumroad eligibility, native follow forms and workflow triggers must be verified in the authenticated seller account. No one should import outside lists without consent. The local HQ does not trigger email sending, subscriber collection or coupon creation.
